This is CS50

CS50 is Harvard University's renowned introductory course to computer science and programming, designed for both beginners and those with prior experience. It emphasizes computational thinking and problem-solving, covering topics like algorithms, data structures, and various programming languages like C and Python. The course is structured around engaging lectures, challenging problem sets, and practical assignments, making it an excellent starting point for anyone interested in computer science. CS50 is well-known for its high production value, passionate instructor, and its ability to make complex concepts accessible to a wide audience.
